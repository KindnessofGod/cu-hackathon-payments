- 2026-11-17 evening: **Answer by voice.** After a request is read out, the app listens for a few seconds (16 kHz WAV, stops after about 1 second of quiet; the recording is not stored) and `/api/voice/listen` sends it to Spitch (Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa) or ElevenLabs Scribe (English, Pidgin), each backing up the other. A word list per language turns the text into pay / refuse / unclear (`src/lib/voice/intent.ts`, unit-tested). "pay" only opens the fingerprint prompt (or, with no fingerprint, asks for a tap); any "no" word refuses, since refusing moves no money; answers longer than 5 words are ignored. Inside the iPad app it starts without a tap; on the website the first tap starts it. iPad app now declares microphone use.
  - Tested on production with a throwaway USD 0.01 request and the app's own hosted audio fed back in: full sentences were transcribed in all five languages in 0.6 to 2.6 s (Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa by Spitch; English, Pidgin by ElevenLabs). The live test showed long sentences can contain "pay" ("you never pay before") or Hausa "biya" inside a negative, which is why answers over 5 words are ignored and "no" words win.
